**Describe a potential investigation you would like to pursue for your Quarter 2 Project.**  
For Quarter 2, I am interested in testing the effect of task diversity on algorithmic transformer training, particularly its influence on the loss plateau. Previous studies have shown that task diversity can reduce the loss plateau in in-context learning scenarios, but this has not been explored in more general contexts. We plan to design experiments where certain shared structures exist between tasks, supporting the idea that task diversity aids training due to these shared structures. These structures may not be obvious and could differ depending on the model. Additionally, we will perform ablation studies to identify factors that positively or negatively influence training dynamics. Understanding these dynamics could guide future ML research toward more computationally efficient and effective models.
